One at a time

What each tool does and who it fits.

Briefly

Briefly works from the firm's whole client record. Statements, inboxes, meeting notes, old plans, Salesforce and Outlook are filed to the right client, and every fact keeps a link to the page it came from. An advisor can ask about one household or the whole book and get numbered sources back. A question like "Which households have mentioned selling a business?" returns the households and the conversations behind them.

Each brief covers what changed since the last meeting, what the client cares about and how the portfolio did. Briefly also drafts agendas, follow-ups, market briefs and prospect analyses, and a prospect analysis runs up to nine checks on the prospect's statements before ending with what it could not verify. With Orion linked, it flags idle cash, drift from each client's model, required distributions and maturities across the book, and one click turns any of them into a Salesforce task. It also prices held-away accounts from their statements. Quarterly reviews and month-end commentary are on the sample deliverables page.

Corrections reach the whole team. One firm fixed a single phrase, "we call it a planning conversation, never a sales meeting," and every brief that firm has received since says it their way. We set Briefly up for your firm, so there is nothing for IT to install, and the web app is $150 per seat per month.

Jump

Jump says 45,000+ advisors across 11,500+ firms use it, and it names LPL, Osaic and Cetera as customers.1, 2 Its Meet plan covers the notetaker, pre-meeting prep, follow-up and CRM sync, and the Onboard add-on handles AI-assisted account opening.3 The AI Associate answers questions across the CRM, plans, portfolio positions and transcripts, the pre-meeting briefing is cited, and Redtail is embedded in a list of 40+ integrations.2, 5, 6, 7 Jump states SOC 2 Type II, and nothing reaches a client or a CRM until an advisor approves it.4 It fits firms that want meeting capture and account opening on one platform.

Zocks

Zocks publishes its prices. Annual plans are $67, $117 and $184 per user per month, with unlimited meetings on all three.12 It says client conversations are never recorded, and it keeps transcripts and summaries that can be deleted on a schedule.13 Client email drafts can be edited before sending or syncing to the mailbox.16 On the Ultimate tier, a document tool reads brokerage statements, estate plans and intake forms and syncs to planning tools such as eMoney and RightCapital.12, 14 Zocks states SOC 2 Type 2 certification from early November 2023.13 It works for advisors who want a published price and planning-tool sync.

Claude

Claude for Financial Advisors, released September 14, 2026, adds eight skills, among them pre-meeting prep, post-meeting notes and follow-up, and prospect intake.20 Anthropic recommends Enterprise plans for registered investment advisers because they include audit logs.20 CRM updates and draft client communications are staged for the advisor to approve.20 Anthropic names SOC 2 and ISO 27001 certifications.23 Firms that want custodian and planning connectors inside Claude are its audience. Firms already on Claude can add Briefly as a connector at $100 per seat per month.

Briefly

Why firms pick Briefly.

Every document goes into the right client's record, and each line in a brief, answer or prospect analysis links to the page it came from, so a reviewer can check any claim against the original.

A correction an advisor makes once becomes a standing rule. Share a client and the colleague gets everything on that client. Firm view turns the CIO's notes into one summary that shows up in the briefs, and scheduled runs email each finished report as a PDF with the firm's logo and two colors on it.

We do the setup. Each firm gets a private environment on AWS. Model calls run on zero-retention terms and are never used for training, and nothing goes into Salesforce until an advisor approves it. At a firm on Orion, idle cash, drift, required distributions and maturities show up across the whole book.

Choosing

How to choose.

  1. If you mainly want a notetaker in the meeting, look at Jump or Zocks.1, 13
  2. If you want briefs, follow-ups, market briefs and prospect analyses written from your whole client record, with a source on every line, look at Briefly.
  3. Firms that want one correction to reach every advisor, with the setup handled for them, also end up on Briefly. If your firm already works in Claude, Briefly also comes as a Claude connector.

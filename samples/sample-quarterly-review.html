PORTFOLIO OVERVIEW

Portfolio Update

U.S. equities rebounded sharply in the second quarter after a weak start to the year, with the S&P 500 gaining roughly 15% for the quarter, its strongest showing since 2020, while broad investment-grade bonds were modestly positive. June itself was choppier: leadership rotated away from the largest technology names, which pulled back meaningfully during the month even as the broader market held its gains.

Against that backdrop, the Calloway portfolio finished June at $267.6 million, up $3.46 million (+1.31%) for the statement period. Every entity ended the month higher. The portfolio's largest exposures, the Omnifund Total Market ETF and NVIDIA, sit at the center of the rotation described above; NVIDIA alone represents 18.6% of investment assets Fabrikam & Northwind statements, Jun 2026, above the 10% single-position threshold Meridian monitors at the relationship level. As discussed at the July 9 review, NVIDIA sales are off the table until the 2024 GRAT term ends in February, so this concentration is a known, dated constraint rather than an open decision.

Concentration

NVIDIA is held in seven of the nine entities and totals $49.7 million, 18.6% of investment assets, above Meridian's 10% single-position monitoring threshold. Where cost basis is stated, the position is deeply appreciated: $18.4M in Holdings LLC (basis $6.31M), $14.2M in the 2024 GRAT (basis $8.67M), $6.1M in the Revocable Trust (basis $2.07M), and $4.8M in the Descendants Trust (basis $0.83M), together roughly $25.6 million of unrealized gain on those four positions alone cost basis, per custodian statements. Per the family's direction at the July 9 review, no NVIDIA sales will be considered before the GRAT term ends in February; diversification paths for the taxable positions (including charitable funding via the CRT and DAF) will be revisited at that point.

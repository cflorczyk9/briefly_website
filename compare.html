The connector and the web app, side by side.

Both read from the same client file, cited and correctable. What changes is where the file lives, who builds it, and where you ask.

Choose the connector if your team already works in Claude and wants files to stay on your own machines. Choose the web app if you want the client file built for you from Salesforce and Outlook. A seat can move from one to the other without rebuilding anything.

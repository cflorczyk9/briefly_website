FAQ

What a seat costs, and how it's billed.

What counts as one seat?

One seat covers one advisor's login, and the whole team shares the same knowledge base behind it. When someone files a note or fixes a detail, every other seat sees the update right away.

How does the free first month work?

The Claude connector's first month doesn't bill. Download it, sign in, and point it at your files. Billing starts in month two at $100 per advisor seat.

How am I billed?

Every plan is priced per advisor seat per month, and your bill moves with how many seats are active.

Can I move a seat or switch plans later?

Yes. Every plan reads from the same knowledge base, so on the Both plan, moving a seat between the connector and the web app doesn't touch your files. Switching your firm from one plan to another works the same way, nothing to rebuild.

What happens if I leave?

With the connector, there's nothing to migrate, since your files were always in your own folder. Removing it just stops Claude from reading that folder. With the web app, we work with you to get your firm's files out of our hosted environment before you go.

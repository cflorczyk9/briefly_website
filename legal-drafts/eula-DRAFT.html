Your Files, Local Processing, and What Brieflywealth Receives

The Connector reads client files from a folder on your own computer. Those files, and any client data within them ("Client Data"), are not uploaded to or stored on Brieflywealth's infrastructure. Content from your files enters an AI model only when you use the Connector inside your own Claude conversation, and that processing runs on the Customer's own Claude or Anthropic plan, under the Customer's own agreement with Anthropic. Brieflywealth does not transmit your Client Data to Anthropic on your behalf. [LEGAL REVIEW: confirm accurate for connector architecture]

What the Connector does send Brieflywealth. To operate the account layer of the Service, the Connector transmits to Brieflywealth: (a) account and sign-in information; (b) seat and entitlement records; (c) usage event records describing each tool call (the tool invoked, the event type, the outcome, seat and firm identifiers, an internal cost figure, and timestamps), which do not include the contents of your files, your prompts, or your Outputs; and (d) de-identified, tokenized preference rules that are stripped of client names, account numbers, and other identifying detail before they leave your computer. This information is handled as described in our Privacy Policy. By using the Connector, you consent to this processing.

Before you install anything

What advisors ask us first.

Is my client data safe?

Your firm's data lives in an environment we secure and control, hosted on AWS with access scoped to your firm, and the firm decides who signs in. Every model call that carries client data uses zero-retention routing, and nothing reaches Salesforce until an advisor approves it. With the Claude connector, files stay on the advisor's computer. Our security page breaks down the full data path if you want more detail.

Do you train AI models on my client data?

We don't use client data to train AI models, and any pattern-level learning runs through a de-identified layer with no client name or account number attached. Model calls that carry client data use zero-retention routing, with a fallback model on the same terms. Our security page covers the details.

Do model providers see my client files?

Briefly sends client data to a model only to answer a question or write a report, and every such call uses zero-retention routing. On the Claude connector, Claude reads the file under your firm's own Claude plan, and on business plans Anthropic's commercial terms bar training on customer content. Our security page lays out the full data path.

Can I put client data into ChatGPT or Claude directly?

Pasting client details into a personal AI chat window sends them to the vendor's servers under consumer terms your firm never reviewed. The SEC's amended Reg S-P rule requires firms to oversee the service providers that handle customer information, which is why compliance teams are asking about AI chat tools. Briefly runs as a web app your firm signs into, with zero-retention routing on every model call that carries client data, so your firm has one vendor to review.

Why not just use Claude or ChatGPT on their own?

AI models change every few months, and what a chat window learned about your firm doesn't carry over. Briefly keeps one current picture of each client, built from your firm's email, notes, transcripts and statements, so the context you build keeps working as models improve or get swapped out. Answers draw on your whole book, corrections hold, and every claim carries a citation you can open.

Can Briefly analyze a prospect before they sign on?

Yes, and you supply everything it works from. Answer five questions about their goals and situation, then attach whatever statements they've shared. Briefly runs up to nine checks against what's actually in those documents, the allocation, the all-in fee, and concentration among them, and hands back findings it can prove alongside questions worth asking at the next meeting. It doesn't make recommendations, and every analysis ends with a plain list of what it could not verify. Read a full sample.

What happens if I leave?

On the web app, your firm has ninety days after leaving to export its files in a standard format, and after that window we delete them. With the Claude connector there is nothing to migrate, because the files were always in your own folders.

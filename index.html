FAQ

Questions advisors ask before they install anything.

Is my client data safe?

With the Briefly connector, client files stay on your computer and Briefly's servers never store them, so there is nothing on our side for anyone at Briefly to see. Only your sign-in, a check that your seat is active, and de-identified preference rules about how you like things written ever reach our server, and we never train AI models on client data. If your firm uses the Briefly web app instead, files live in Briefly's hosted environment, which we secure and control, and your firm decides who signs in.

Can I safely put client data into ChatGPT or Claude directly?

Pasting client details into a general AI chat window sends them to the vendor's servers under the vendor's own terms, the kind of outside data handling securities regulators expect firms to review. There is a cleaner setup. With the Briefly connector, client files stay on your computer, and Claude works from them under your firm's own Claude plan.

Which one should I pick?

If your team already works in Claude, get the connector. It reads folders on your own machines and your advisors ask questions right where they already work. If you want the client file built for you, pick the web app. We connect Salesforce and Outlook and your team signs in from a browser. A seat can move between them without rebuilding anything.

Why not just use Claude on its own?

On its own, Claude forgets your firm when the chat ends. Briefly gives it a client file that persists. A correction one advisor makes becomes a standing rule the whole team inherits, answers can draw on your entire book instead of one conversation, and every claim carries a citation you can open. None of that lives in a single chat window.

How is Briefly different from a CRM?

A CRM keeps a record of activity. Briefly reads what your firm already stores, including CRM records, and turns it into one knowledge base you can question in plain English. Firms keep Salesforce for workflow and use Briefly to find and use what the firm already knows.

What does Briefly cost?

The web app is $150 per advisor seat per month. The Claude connector is $100, and your first month on it is free. Running both is $175. Every plan is the same knowledge base with the same citations, and what changes is where your client files live.

What happens if I leave?

It depends on your setup. With the connector, there's nothing to migrate, since your files were always in your own folder, and removing the connector just stops Claude from reading it. With the web app, we work with you to get your firm's files out of our hosted environment before you go.

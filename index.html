Before you install anything

What advisors ask us first.

Is my client data safe?

With the connector, client files stay on your computer and our servers never store them, so there is nothing on our side for anyone to see. We never train AI models on client data. Firms that choose the hosted version keep their files in an environment we secure and control, and the firm decides who signs in.

Can I put client data into ChatGPT or Claude?

Pasting client details into a general AI chat window sends them to the vendor's servers under the vendor's own terms, the kind of outside data handling regulators expect firms to review. With the Briefly connector, client files stay on your computer and Claude works from them under your firm's own plan.

Why not just use Claude or ChatGPT on their own?

A chat window forgets your firm when the conversation ends. Briefly gives Claude a client file that persists on your machines, so answers draw on your whole book, corrections hold, and every claim carries a citation you can open.

Which one should I pick?

If your team already works in Claude, start with the connector. It reads folders on your own machines and answers where you already work. If you want the file built for you, take the web app. We connect Salesforce and Outlook and your team signs in from a browser. A seat can move between them without rebuilding anything.

How long does setup take?

The connector installs like any Claude connector and reads the folders you point it at, so most teams are asking questions the same day. The hosted version takes longer because we connect Salesforce and Outlook and build the client file for you.

What happens if I leave?

With the connector there is nothing to migrate, because the files were always in your own folders. Removing it just stops Claude from reading them. On the hosted version, we get your firm's files out to you before you go.
